# Using Dropper for Twitch

[Back to the README](../README.md)

## Install and open

1. Install a userscript manager such as [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/get-it/) or [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
2. [Install Dropper for Twitch](https://github.com/ExtraPotions/Dropper/releases/latest/download/dropper.user.js) and confirm in your userscript manager.
3. Sign in to Twitch, refresh, and open the Dropper launcher in Twitch's top bar. With other ExtraPotions products installed, they share one launcher: open it and choose Dropper.

The script appears as **Dropper for Twitch** in your userscript manager.

Stream switching starts at **Follow Campaign Order** in the first eligible tab. Saved choices, manual stream holds and pauses are respected; additional browsing tabs stay where you open them. Change the policy in **Settings → Stream Switching**.

## Features and controls

- **Track progress:** see credited watch time for the current reward on a compact card. On a channel page the card sits in place of the Stream Chat title bar, and the menu opens from its edge. Turn off **Look → Drop Details** for a one-line card with the channel, game and percentage. **Hide Chat** on the card collapses Twitch chat, and Twitch's Expand Chat reopens it. Pause or Resume reflects your viewing intent; Skip asks you to press it again. Twitch decides eligibility and credit.
- **Check eligibility:** see whether the stream you are watching matches the campaign: account, campaign, reward, game, and stream.
- **Keep rewards in reach:** browse campaigns, review your history, and open your Drops inventory from the menu.
- **Choose your games:** follow the games you care about and ignore the rest.
- **Site theme:** choose Slate Blue Light, Dark or System in **Look → Site Theme**. System follows your device. Twitch's functional purchase, live and status colors keep their meaning.

## Shared menu

Each section is a drawer: opening one closes the others, and the menu reopens on the one you used last. Open **System** for status, diagnostics, troubleshooting and Reset. Menu sizing is in **Look**. With multiple ExtraPotions products on the same page, open the shared launcher and choose a product; on Twitch each product has its own icon in the top bar. Donations are available from the heart on the progress card.

## Look

The menu is charcoal with purple highlights, in Light, Dark and System. System follows your device and updates when its appearance changes. Site theme uses Slate Blue with the same three choices and is independent of the menu. Legacy palette settings migrate to System. Gem themes, palette choices and custom menu opacity are no longer available.
