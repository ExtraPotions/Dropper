# Dropper

**Follow your Twitch Drops from watch time to reward.**

## Get started

1. Install a userscript manager such as [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/get-it/) or [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
2. [Install Dropper](https://github.com/ExtraPotions/Dropper/releases/latest/download/dropper.user.js) and confirm in your userscript manager.
3. Sign in to Twitch, refresh, and open the Dropper launcher.

## What you can do

- **Track progress:** see credited watch time for the current reward. Twitch decides eligibility and credit.
- **Claim automatically:** optionally claim earned Drops and collect channel-point bonus chests.
- **Stay on an eligible stream:** Dropper checks streams against Twitch's campaign details and moves on when a stream goes offline, changes category, or stops earning.
- **Set the rules:** quiet hours, protected or excluded channels, and which games to follow or ignore.
